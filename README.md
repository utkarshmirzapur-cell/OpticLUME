## Overview

OpticLUME is a desktop optical engineering platform developed to bridge the gap between learning, rapid prototyping, and research-grade optical simulation.

The project integrates multiple domains of optics into a single native application, allowing engineers, researchers, and students to design, analyze, and validate optical systems without relying on numerous disconnected tools.

Rather than focusing on one simulation technique, OpticLUME combines several optical disciplines under one interface, creating a unified workflow for optical design and experimentation.

---

## Current Capabilities

- Optical Material Database
- Fourier Optics
- Beam Propagation
- Fresnel & Diffractive Optics
- Thin Film Analysis
- Grating Design
- Computational Holography
- Photonic Device Design
- RCWA Simulation
- Native 2D & 3D FDTD
- Polarization & Jones Calculus
- Waveguide Design
- Meta-Optics Design
- AR Waveguide Design
- Parameter Sweep & Optimization
- Project Versioning
- Benchmark Library

---

## Design Philosophy

OpticLUME follows a simple philosophy:

> Learn → Design → Simulate → Optimize → Validate

The objective is to provide an integrated engineering workflow while remaining lightweight, transparent, and extensible.

## Motivation

OpticLUME began as a personal R&D initiative to consolidate optical engineering workflows into a single modular platform.

The long-term vision is to build an extensible ecosystem covering classical optics, computational optics, diffractive optics, photonics, and advanced optical system design.
